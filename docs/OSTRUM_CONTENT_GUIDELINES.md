# Ostrum — Global Content & Copywriting Guidelines

> **Status:** RATIFIED (Phase 01 Global Refinement)  
> **Mandate:** Simple Words • Strong Ideas • Human Language • Business Outcomes  
> **Target Reader:** Business Owners, Operations Leaders, Founders, School Trustees, Commercial Directors  

---

## 1. The Core Content Principle

> **SIMPLE WORDS. STRONG IDEAS. HUMAN LANGUAGE. BUSINESS OUTCOMES.**  
> **TECHNICAL DEPTH ONLY WHERE GENUINELY USEFUL.**

A visitor should understand what Ostrum does without needing to know software architecture, cloud jargon, API terminology, or AI hype phrases. 

When a business owner visits the Ostrum website, they should think:  
**“These people understand my daily headaches, speak in plain English, and build technology that will actually make my company run better.”**

## 2. Voice & Tone Profile

| Dimension | How Ostrum Sounds | What Ostrum Never Sounds Like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clarity** | Direct, plain-spoken, crisp sentences. | Vague consulting slide decks; 40-word run-on sentences. |
| **Confidence** | Calm, measured, experienced. | Hype-driven, sensational, desperate ("Revolutionary!"). |

| **Warmth** | Approachable, collaborative, human. | Cold, sterile, robotic enterprise IT vendor. |

## 3. Vocabulary Matrix: Words to Ban vs. Words to Use

### Jargon Words Strictly Banned Across Headlines & Primary Copy
* ❌ *Architectonic / Topological / Telemetry / Synchronicity*
* ❌ *Autonomous orchestration / Agentic pipeline execution*
* ❌ *Headless omnichannel commerce paradigm*
* ❌ *Unified spend-to-revenue attribution modeling*
* ❌ *Revolutionary / Disruptive / Cutting-edge / Next-generation*
* ❌ *Synergy / Paradigm shift / Holistic digital transformation*
* ❌ *Zero human intervention required* (sounds scary and inaccurate)
* ❌ Repeating *"Digital ecosystem"* three times per paragraph

### Plain Human Words to Prefer Instead
* ✅ **Build / Connect / Organize / Automate / Run / Grow**
* ✅ **Tools / Software / Systems / Websites / Stores / Dashboards**
* ✅ **Busywork / Manual work / Spreadsheets / Double data entry**
* ✅ **Save time / Close more sales / Answer customers faster / Avoid mistakes**
* ✅ **Clear plan / Step-by-step / Friendly support / Reliable code**

## 5. How to Discuss AI Without Hype or Fear

1. **Focus on the Task, Not the Model:** Talk about what the assistant *does* (e.g. *"sends the prospectus PDF to the parent within 10 seconds"*, *"checks which store has a medium linen shirt in stock"*), not the LLM parameter size or token windows.
2. **Never Promise "Zero Human Intervention":** Real businesses want automation that *empowers* their staff, not an unmonitored rogue bot. Say: *"Handles routine repetitive inquiries so your team can focus on complex customer conversations."*
3. **Be Concrete:** Show real inputs (a customer's WhatsApp message) and real outputs (an updated CRM row and a calendar invite).

---

## 6. How to Present Case Studies & Proof Truthfully

* **Strict Ban on Fabricated Proof:** Never invent client names, fake 5-star ratings, synthetic review quotes, or exaggerated metrics (*"Increased sales by 850% in 3 days!"*).
* **Clear Labeling:** If an example is a concept, prototype, or internal demonstration, label it honestly:
  - `Demonstration Project`
  - `Client Concept`
  - `Architecture Case Study`
* **Focus on Problem and Solution:** Describe the operational friction before Ostrum, what was designed and built, and what changed in the client's day-to-day operations.

---

## 7. The 5-Second Comprehension Test

Before approving any headline or paragraph on the Ostrum website, test it against these three questions:

1. **Can a busy founder read this once and repeat what it means to their partner?**
2. **Does it sound like something a real human would say across a coffee table?**
3. **Does it explain a benefit to the business, rather than showing off our vocabulary?**

If any answer is "No", rewrite it.
