### Chapter 03: What We Solve / Business Problem
*   **Purpose:** Direct empathy for daily operational chaos.
*   **Headline:** "You don't need more software. You need your software to talk to each other."
*   **The 6 Real Problems:**
    1. *Lost Inquiries:* Leads buried in email inboxes before anyone replies.
    2. *Spreadsheet Chaos:* Orders, staff, and inventory trapped in conflicting Excel files.
    3. *Scattered Messages:* Customer sales handled on staff personal WhatsApp numbers.
    4. *Double Data Entry:* Typing the same numbers three times into billing and accounting.
    5. *Chatbots That Annoy:* Generic bots that can't look up an order or answer real questions.
    6. *Blind Decisions:* Business owners spending weekends compiling revenue reports.

### Chapter 04: The Ostrum Approach / Connected System
*   **Purpose:** Present Ostrum’s 5-layer unified model.
*   **Headline:** "One connected system for your entire business."
*   **Layers:**
    *   `01. Brand & Creative:` Visual identity and clear messaging that earns trust immediately.
    *   `02. Websites & Digital Stores:` Fast, high-conversion web applications and online shops.
    *   `03. Custom Business Systems:` Tailored inventory, billing, student portals, and CRM pipelines.
    *   `04. Helpful AI & Automation:` Smart assistants qualifying leads and dispatching paperwork 24/7.
    *   `05. Clear Business Dashboards:` Real-time view of revenue, orders, and leads on one screen.
