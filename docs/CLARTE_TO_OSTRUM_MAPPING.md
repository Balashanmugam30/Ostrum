## 2. Harmonizing Clarté's Craft with Ostrum's 17-Chapter Narrative

Clarté is a focused 5-section narrative about an experiential book.  
Ostrum is a comprehensive, modern digital systems and transformation studio website.  

To adapt Clarté’s visual discipline to Ostrum’s comprehensive business scope without losing purity:
1. **Pacing Chapters as Scenes:** Rather than stacking 17 generic rectangular cards, each section is composed as an intentional scene with distinct surface shifts (Warm Alabaster Linen → Pearl Porcelain → Misty Slate → Crisp Card White → Warm Bisque).
2. **Typography as Hero:** Sections like Philosophy, Studio Genesis, and the Final Inquiry use monumental typography, whitespace, and optical serif highlights to command attention without needing decorative clutter.
3. **Interactive Demonstration Layers:** The 3D architectural object, the live automation sandbox, the interactive capability switcher, and the 4-step project brief provide rich tactile interaction.
