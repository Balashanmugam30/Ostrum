## 1. Problem Analysis & Owner Findings

In the previous revision, the OSTRUM favicon was generated with a solid dark burgundy plate behind the symbol. In the owner's browser tab comparison screenshot (`image(20261110-061841).png`), this resulted in:
1. **Tiny Pixel Occupancy:** The symbol occupied less than 40% of the small 16×16 px tab canvas because the dark plate swallowed the outer boundaries.
2. **Indistinct Silhouette:** In contrast to the neighbouring ChatGPT tab icon (which uses a transparent alpha channel and occupies ~90% of the canvas), the OSTRUM icon appeared small, murky, and lacked definition.
3. **Improper Contrast on Dark/Light OS Themes:** The solid dark plate failed to integrate naturally with different browser tab themes.

---

## 2. Definitive Solution: Pure Transparent 3D Sculpture Master

### 2.1 Authoritative 3D Source Asset
Rather than using an AI approximation or low-resolution screenshot, the master symbol was generated by directly rendering the **actual production 3D model** powering the OSTRUM hero experience:
- **Geometry Asset:** `public/models/monyedre-360.glb` (116,726 triangles, 61,238 vertices).
- **Material Shader:** Exact Crimson-Porcelain Physical Material from `components/visuals/OstrumContinuousJourney.tsx`:
  - `roughness: 0.26`, `metalness: 0.06`
  - `clearcoat: 0.58`, `clearcoatRoughness: 0.18`
  - `sheen: 0.65`, `sheenColor: #ffdcd8`, `sheenRoughness: 0.35`
  - `specularColor: #ffffff`, `specularIntensity: 0.95`
  - `iridescence: 0.06`, `iridescenceIOR: 1.32`
- **Lighting Rig:** 4-point studio rig matching the hero resting scene:
  - Key Directional Light (`#fff7ee`, 2.4 intensity) at `(-2.5, 3.6, 3.2)`
  - Ambient Hemisphere Light (`#fdeddc` / `#220404`, 1.0 intensity)
  - Subtle Rim Point Light (`#ff4d30`, 2.0 intensity) at `(3.4, -1.2, -2.6)`
  - Fill Directional Light (`#ffebd8`, 0.55 intensity) at `(1.8, 1.2, 2.0)`
- **Orientation:** Exact resting rotation when docked inside the Hero 'O':
  - `pivotGroup.rotation.x = 0.20`
  - `pivotGroup.rotation.z = -0.05`
  - `pivotGroup.rotation.y = 0.0`

### 2.2 100% Alpha Transparency & 92% Canvas Occupancy
- Rendered via Chromium WebGL offscreen renderer with `alpha: true` and `setClearColor(0x000000, 0)`.
- No dark square plate, no circular badge, no burgundy box, and no baked-in lettering.
- Camera framed tightly with perspective compensation so the sculpted silhouette fills **92.0% of the square canvas**:
  - `minX: 41, maxX: 982` (width: 942 px)
  - `minY: 93, maxY: 931` (height: 839 px)
  - Total non-zero alpha pixel count: 506,330 pixels.
