## 1. Search Intent & Commercial Strategy Overview

OSTRUM's commercial model serves two complementary buyer journeys:
1. **OSTRUM Studio (Custom Engineering & Digital Solutions):** Serving businesses, startups, and enterprises seeking high-reliability custom software, modern web and mobile applications, practical AI systems and workflow automations, API integrations, and connected systems. Direct builder access without agency intermediaries.
2. **OSTRUM Ventures (Product Incubation & Labs):** Building and scaling high-conviction internal ventures (such as MNESA for intelligent opportunity capture), with selective engineering partnerships for ambitious ideas.

### Search Intent Archetypes:
- **Commercial Investigation:** Tech-forward founders and enterprise decision-makers in India and abroad looking for an engineering partner capable of full-cycle architecture, modern stack engineering (Next.js, TypeScript, Python, Cloud), and AI workflow integration.
- **Problem-Led Solutions:** Organizations needing to modernize legacy operations, automate manual bottlenecks with AI agents, or build custom business portals rather than purchasing rigid, expensive SaaS.
- **Technical Validation:** Prospective clients evaluating technical depth, engineering integrity, past delivery evidence (TMR AI Car Care, Saraswati Driving School), and security consciousness.
