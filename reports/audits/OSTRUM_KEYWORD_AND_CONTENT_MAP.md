## 1. Search Intent & Commercial Strategy Overview

OSTRUM's commercial model serves two complementary buyer journeys:
1. **OSTRUM Studio (Custom Engineering & Digital Solutions):** Serving businesses, startups, and enterprises seeking high-reliability custom software, modern web and mobile applications, UI/UX design, e-commerce platforms, practical AI systems and workflow automations, API integrations, and digital growth/SEO. Direct access to engineering principals without agency account managers.
2. **OSTRUM Ventures (Product Incubation & Labs):** Building and scaling high-conviction internal ventures (such as MNESA for intelligent opportunity capture), with selective engineering partnerships for ambitious ideas.

### Recommended Keyword Targeting Tiers:

#### Tier 1: Primary India-Wide Commercial Themes
- **Custom software development company in India:** custom business software, bespoke software development, enterprise software development (Hero, FOR BUSINESS copy, Software & Product Engineering card, FAQ)
- **AI development company in India:** AI agent development, AI automation, AI workflow automation, AI integrations (Hero supporting copy, AI Systems & Agents card, FAQ)
- **Website design and development:** web development, web applications, customer portals, UI/UX design (Digital Experiences card, FAQ)
- **Digital product engineering:** SaaS development, MVP development, product engineering, API development (Hero, Software & Product Engineering card)
- **Digital marketing services in India:** SEO services, content strategy, performance marketing (Digital Experiences card, FAQ, contact project-type selection)

#### Tier 2: Specific Service Intent
- E-commerce website development
- UI/UX design for web applications
- Mobile app development
- CRM and ERP integration services
- API integration services
- Custom operations software
- Legacy application modernization
- Business process automation
- IoT development and embedded software
- Startup MVP development
- Venture studio and venture building

#### Tier 3: Problem-Led Long-Tail Intent
- Custom software to automate business operations
- Software to replace manual spreadsheets and disconnected tools
- Custom CRM or operations platform for a growing business
- AI automation for customer enquiries and internal workflows
- Build a SaaS MVP from idea to launch
- Integrate existing business software through APIs
- Modernize a legacy business application
- Website, SEO and digital growth support for a business
